## MIT license with a generic copyright holder

DevBot is released under the MIT license, with the copyright line held generically by "DevBot contributors" rather than a named legal entity. The requirement stated was "do whatever you want, but a product based on it must give attribution" — MIT matches exactly, granting unrestricted commercial and derivative use while requiring the copyright and permission notice be preserved in copies and substantial portions. The holder line was initially going to be omitted and was kept deliberately: it is the very thing a derivative must reproduce, so without it the attribution condition cannot bind anyone and licence-detection tooling (GitHub licensee, SPDX scanners) stops recognising the file as MIT. The holder is left generic to avoid naming a legal entity that does not exist yet — switch to a specific holder if one is ever established.
