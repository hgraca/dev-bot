## k8s-infra chart does NOT bundle kube-state-metrics

The SigNoz k8s-infra Helm chart v0.16.0 does not include kube-state-metrics as a dependency (verified by reading chart templates directory). KSM must be deployed separately (e.g. `prometheus-community/kube-state-metrics` Helm chart). Configure the k8s-infra chart's `prometheus` preset with a `scrapeConfigs` entry pointing to the KSM service. Moving from custom collectors to k8s-infra: keep KSM as a separate ArgoCD Application — do not expect the k8s-infra chart to provide it.
