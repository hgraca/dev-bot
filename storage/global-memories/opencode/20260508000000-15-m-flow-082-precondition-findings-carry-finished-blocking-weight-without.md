## M-FLOW-082: Precondition findings carry [FINISHED]-blocking weight without being typed as BLOCKERs

improve-planning iter-9. The FINAL-promotion gate at `src/agents/devbot.md:162` (introduced iter-7 P2) read: "Only the orchestrator may transition `Status` to `FINAL`, and only after a critic round whose verdict is `APPROVED` or whose findings contain zero `BLOCKER`s." In iter-9, R2's verdict was `CONDITIONALLY APPROVED` with zero BLOCKERs, but Check C ("Pre-[FINISHED] Hygiene Gate not executed") was flagged as **a precondition for [FINISHED], not for review approval** (R2 line 177). The orchestrator interpreted "zero BLOCKERs" as sufficient and promoted to FINAL. Literal text satisfied; spirit violated. The critic's "precondition" terminology is deliberate — preconditions are [FINISHED]-time checks that don't fit the BLOCKER/WEAKNESS/SUGGESTION severity tiers because they're not about plan correctness, they're about plan-completion gates being executed before promotion. Fix (iter-9 P1): FINAL-gate now requires verdict `APPROVED` AND zero BLOCKERs AND zero unmet preconditions. `CONDITIONALLY APPROVED` is forbidden as a FINAL-promotion path; the orchestrator MUST resolve the precondition (re-delegate to architect, then re-delegate to critic for confirmation) before promotion.
When a gate condition uses a severity threshold ("zero X" where X is a typed severity), audit whether other finding categories carry equivalent blocking weight under different names. Severity labels capture _correctness defects_ (BLOCKER = wrong, WEAKNESS = weak, SUGGESTION = optional). They don't capture _process gates_ (precondition = required step not executed, prerequisite = upstream artefact missing, dependency = blocked on external work). These are [FINISHED]-blocking on equivalent footing but invisible to severity-counting rules. Design test for any gate: enumerate every finding category the upstream role can produce; for each, ask "does this carry [FINISHED]-blocking weight?"; for each that does, confirm it's listed at the gate. Generalises: this is the same defect shape as iter-7 P1 ("rule fires syntactically but semantic predicate isn't checked") and iter-8 P2 ("verification verifies _a_ property, not _the_ property"). The pattern: gates expressed in terms of one observable (severity count, file existence) miss other observables that carry equivalent semantic weight.
